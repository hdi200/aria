PaddleOCR PP-OCRv6 (chord and text readers)

Aria's Scan reads chord names with PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv6 small text recognition model, fine-tuned on chord
symbols by the ScoreScanner project and exported to ONNX (ScannerCore's chord_rec.onnx), and reads the title,
composer, tempo and staff text with the same model as released, exported to ONNX (text_rec.onnx).

PaddleOCR and its PP-OCRv6 models: Apache License 2.0, copyright PaddlePaddle Authors,
https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR. The license text is in copisteria-Apache-2.0.txt (the Apache License
2.0). The chord reader's weights are a modified version: they were trained further on synthetic chord-symbol
images. The text reader's weights are unmodified.
