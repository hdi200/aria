## Compose bridge (skip.ui ComposeBuilder, View.Compose, layouts) and every aria.skip view
## ran interpreted or JIT-compiled after each install and update: about 70% of the main
## thread's editing time was JIT code or the ART interpreter on a Galaxy A35
## (MuseReaderAndroid/docs/archive/GALAXY_A35_EDIT_PROFILE.md, finding #3).
